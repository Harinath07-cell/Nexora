## 🧮 The Magic: Mathematical Analysis

### Financial Metrics (Calculated Automatically)

| Metric | Formula | Benchmark | Your Score |
|--------|---------|-----------|------------|
| **Gross Profit Margin** | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100 | 15-25% | ✅ Calculated |
| **Net Profit Margin** | Net Profit / Revenue × 100 | 10-20% | ✅ Calculated |
| **Operating Profit Margin** | Operating Profit / Revenue × 100 | 12-18% | ✅ Calculated |
| **Debt-to-Equity Ratio** | Total Debt / Total Equity | < 2.0 | ✅ Calculated |
| **Current Ratio** | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | > 1.5 | ✅ Calculated |
| **Return on Investment** | Annual Profit / Investment × 100 | > 15% | ✅ Calculated |

### Operational Metrics (Auto-Calculated)

| Metric | Formula | Benchmark | Your Score |
|--------|---------|-----------|------------|
| **Revenue per Employee** | Monthly Revenue / Employees | > ₹50,000 | ✅ Calculated |
| **Revenue per Sq Ft** | Monthly Revenue / Area | > ₹200/sq ft | ✅ Calculated |
| **Capacity Utilization** | Actual / Max Capacity × 100 | 70-85% | ✅ Calculated |
| **Daily Revenue** | Customers × Avg Transaction | - | ✅ Calculated |

### Health Scores (0-100 Scale)

```
Overall Health = (Financial Health + Operational Health + Market Health) / 3

Where:
- Financial Health (33%) = Profit margins + Debt ratio + Liquidity
- Operational Health (33%) = Efficiency + Productivity + Utilization
- Market Health (33%) = Competition + Location + Customer base
```
